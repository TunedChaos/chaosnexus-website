# Support the Project

ChaosNexus is AGPL open source from Tuned Chaos. Sustaining it takes time on sandboxing, the Forge IDE, and local model work.

I am a solo developer. Support helps keep releases shipping. Support for ChaosNexus goes through Tuned Chaos, the studio behind it, and every option is listed on the [Tuned Chaos supporters page](https://tunedchaos.com/supporters/). There is no SLA on sponsorships or donations. For paid work, email **commercial@tunedchaos.com**.
